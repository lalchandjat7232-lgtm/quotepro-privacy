QuotePro Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

App: QuotePro
Developer: QuotePro

1. Introduction

QuotePro is a quotation-making application that helps users create, manage, generate and share professional quotations. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

2. Information stored by QuotePro

QuotePro can store information entered by the user, including business information, customer details, quotation details, business logo, digital signature, and app settings.

  • Business name, owner name, phone number, email address, address, GSTIN and website.
  • Business logo and digital signature supplied by the user.
  • Customer details entered by the user.
  • Quotation numbers, dates, items, quantities, rates, discounts, taxes and totals.
  • App settings and quotation preferences.

This information is primarily stored locally on the user's device. QuotePro does not require users to create an account.

3. How information is used

User-entered information is used to create quotations, calculate totals and taxes, maintain quotation history, generate PDFs, and prepare documents for sharing.

4. PDF generation and sharing

When the user chooses to generate or share a quotation, QuotePro creates the requested document and uses the device's available sharing functionality. The user controls the recipient or third-party application used for sharing.

5. Advertising

The free version of QuotePro may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These services may collect or use certain device, advertising and usage information according to their own privacy policies. QuotePro does not sell the user's quotation database or business records.

6. In-app purchases

QuotePro may offer paid Pro features through Google Play billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. QuotePro does not receive or store the user's full payment card details.

7. Data sharing

QuotePro does not sell users' personal information. Quotation information is shared with third parties when the user chooses a device sharing feature or when a third-party service is required to provide an app feature, such as advertising or Google Play billing.

8. Data security

QuotePro is designed to keep quotation and business information in local app storage on the user's device. Users should keep their device protected with appropriate device security. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because quotation and business information is stored locally, users can remove it using the app's available delete or clear functions and by uninstalling the app. Users should export or save information they want to keep before deleting local app data or uninstalling the app.

QuotePro does not currently provide user accounts, so there is no QuotePro account to delete.

10. Children's privacy

QuotePro is a business productivity application and is not directed specifically to children.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when QuotePro's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available through the published privacy policy page.
